# Governance

Published versions are immutable. Corrections create patch releases and preserve prior manifests through vintage_id and supersedes_id.

A normalizer extracts atomic claims. A verifier reconstructs them from first-party sources and checks timestamps. A release owner confirms contract, manifest and CI status. Material methodology changes increment the protocol version.

Prefer filings, issuer releases and prepared remarks. Secondary reporting may discover evidence but does not replace an available primary source. Management forecasts remain issuer_claim even when numerically precise.

Use public information only. Respect publisher terms and redistribution rights. Research claims must identify protocol, manifest, price source, universe, costs, period and holdout status. A registered falsifier is reported as such rather than reframed through unregistered variants.
